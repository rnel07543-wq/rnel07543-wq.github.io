Questions Couples Ask

Do you offer photography and cinematography together?

Yes. Our Unified collections bring photography and cinematography together as one cohesive visual story, from the nervous excitement before the ceremony to the last dance. Photography-only and cinematography-only collections are also available.

How many photographers and cinematographers will be at our wedding?

SkyRidge Collective is built around our two co-founders — Rynhardt behind the camera for film, Christo behind the lens for photography. A second shooter can be added to any collection if you’d like additional coverage.

How far do you travel?

We’re based in Pretoria and travel throughout South Africa — including Johannesburg, Cape Town and Durban — as well as internationally for destination weddings. Tell us where your day is happening and we’ll confirm travel arrangements as part of your enquiry.

Can we customise a collection?

Every collection can be tailored to your needs. Popular add-ons include drone coverage, extra edited photos, a longer feature film, a social media reels package, a second shooter and a same-day edit — speak to us about building a package that fits your vision exactly.

How do we secure our date?

Get in touch through our enquiry form or WhatsApp with your wedding date and details. We reply within 24 hours to confirm availability and walk you through next steps.
